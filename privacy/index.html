5. Secure DNS feature

Secure DNS is an optional feature you turn on yourself. When enabled, the App uses the Android VpnService interface only to send your device's DNS lookups (the step that turns a website name into an address) to Cloudflare's public DNS resolver at 1.1.1.1 and 1.0.0.1. Cloudflare processes those DNS queries under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy and its 1.1.1.1 resolver privacy information.

Important limits of this feature:

  • It is not a full VPN. It does not route your web traffic through a remote VPN server, does not encrypt your web traffic, and does not hide your IP address. Websites, ad networks and your internet provider can still see your IP address.
  • We do not operate the DNS resolver and we do not receive or log your DNS queries.
  • While Secure DNS is on, Android shows a persistent notification and the key icon in the status bar, because the feature runs as a foreground service. Turn it off in the App at any time to stop it.

6. Ad Block, Anonymity Shield and incognito tabs

  • Ad Block filters requests to known advertising hosts inside the App's browser only; it does not affect other apps or the advertisements that support the App itself. To keep the filter current, the App downloads a public list of ad-server host names from pgl.yoyo.org (Peter Lowe's ad server list). That request reveals your IP address and standard connection information to that site.
  • Anonymity Shield, when on, blocks screenshots and screen recording of the App and clears cookies and web storage when it is turned on or off. It does not hide your IP address or make you anonymous on the internet.
  • Incognito tabs do not save browsing history in the App. Websites, your network, your internet provider and the SDKs described above can still observe your activity; files you download remain on your device until you delete them.
