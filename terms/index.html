3. What the App does

Swift Browser is a web browser. It lets you open websites, search with the search engine you select, manage tabs (including incognito tabs), bookmarks, history and downloads, and use optional tools:

  • Secure DNS sends your device's DNS lookups to Cloudflare's public resolvers (1.1.1.1 and 1.0.0.1) using Android's VpnService interface. It is not a full VPN, does not hide your IP address and does not encrypt your web traffic. A notification is shown while it is on.

  • Anonymity Shield blocks screenshots and screen recording in the App and clears cookies and web storage when toggled. It does not make you anonymous and does not hide your IP address.

These tools are provided to help you manage your own privacy preferences; none of them guarantees security, privacy or anonymity.
