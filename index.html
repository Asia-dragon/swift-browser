Ad Block

Fewer ads, cleaner pages.

Turn on Ad Block to filter ad and pop-up requests while you browse in Swift. It works inside this browser only, and some sites may still show ads.

Secure DNS

Safer DNS lookups in one tap.

Secure DNS sends your DNS queries through Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) to help block unsafe sites and reduce DNS tracking. It is not a full VPN: it does not hide your IP address or encrypt your website traffic.

Anonymity Shield

A quieter screen and a clean session.

Anonymity Shield blocks screenshots and screen recording inside the app and clears cookies and cache when you switch it on or off. It does not hide your IP address.

FAQ
Is Swift Browser free?

It is free to download. The app contains ads, shown with your consent choices where required.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign-in.

Is Secure DNS a VPN?

No. Secure DNS uses Android's VPN service only as a local tunnel to send DNS queries to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1). It does not hide your IP address or encrypt your website traffic.

Does Ad Block work in other apps?

No. Ad Block filters ad and pop-up requests inside Swift Browser only, and some sites may still show ads.

Where is my browsing history stored?

On your device. You can clear tabs, history, cookies and cache any time from Settings.
